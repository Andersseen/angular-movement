# Code of Conduct

angular-movement should be a useful, respectful place for Angular developers of different
experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, especially across language and experience gaps.
- Keep feedback specific to the code, docs, behavior, or proposal.
- Welcome questions from people who are learning Angular, animation, or open source.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Dismissive comments about someone's background, identity, language, or skill level.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask to move on.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that makes the project unsafe or unwelcoming, contact the maintainer through
the security contact in `SECURITY.md` or open a private channel if one is listed on the GitHub
repository.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, block users, or take other moderation action
when needed.
